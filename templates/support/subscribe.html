Common questions

What can I try for free?

Our proposed free preview includes Lessons 1–3. A paid plan would unlock Lesson 4 and all remaining lessons in the course.

What does the Individual plan include?

The proposed Individual plan gives one learner access to all course lessons, personal progress, points and avatar customization.

How many people can use the Family plan?

The proposed Family plan includes up to 5 family members, each with their own progress, plus family challenges and shared milestones.

What does unlimited mean for organizations?

The proposed Organization plan covers learners within a single school, club or organization. Separate organizations would need separate plans.

Can I subscribe now?

Not yet. This page previews our planned subscriptions. Payments and paid access are not connected in this demo.

Does this page collect payment information?

No. This demo does not collect card details or process payments.
